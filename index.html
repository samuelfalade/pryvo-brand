The small business toolkit. Pricing, invoices and bookkeeping for solo service businesses, on iOS.

Mark

A green receipt card on warm off-white, torn along the bottom, with the P reversed out and a single amber total line under it. The line is the tagline, cut out of the bar in the card colour: a bar at icon size, readable up close.

Lockup

Wordmark in SF Pro Rounded Semibold, lowercase, tracked tight. The tagline is the icon's bar scaled under "ryvo", with the words cut out so the page shows through. Beneath the p's tail sits a foot block whose cradle is the tail's own end mirrored.

Rules

  • Clear space of one mark-height on every side.
  • Minimum size: icon 24 px, wordmark 100 px wide.
  • Never stretch, outline, add a drop shadow, or set the mark on a photo.
  • The bar is always amber and always carries the tagline. Nothing else goes on the card.

Palette

Ink on paper is about 7.6:1, passing AA for text and AAA for large text. Amber is the one accent on the mark.

Type

Wordmark: SF Pro Rounded Semibold. Tagline on the mark: SF Pro Rounded Black capitals, tracked tight. In the app: SF Pro at Dynamic Type sizes, tabular figures wherever a number appears.
