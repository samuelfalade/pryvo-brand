The toolkit

Three jobs, one app, one set of numbers.

Price, invoice and keep the books from the same records, so the margin you see on the quote is the margin you see at tax time.

01 — Price

A floor under every quote

Enter your yearly business costs and salary target once. Pryvo spreads them across the jobs you plan to do, adds each job type's own costs and shows the least you can charge. Set a profit margin and it warns you when a quote falls below it.

How it works

Set it up in an evening. Quote with it for years.

Enter your costs

Yearly business expenses and the salary you need to take home. Mileage rate, tax rate and currency live in settings.

Describe your job types

A project template per kind of work, each with its own costs and products. Pryvo turns them into a price floor per job.

Quote with the floor in view

Create a project for a client, see its cost and margin as you price it, and send the quote. Expiring quotes and overdue payments raise a notification.

Invoice and reconcile

Convert the quote, log payments, import your statement and export the year for your accountant.

Private by design

Your books stay yours.

Client names and bank lines are sensitive. Pryvo is built so they do not have to leave the phone.

Face ID lock and a privacy coverLock the app behind Face ID, Touch ID or a PIN. The screen is covered the moment you switch apps.
AI that runs on the deviceAsk about your numbers using Apple Intelligence on-device. Cloud and bring-your-own-key options exist, and the app says plainly what each one sends.
An open backup fileBack up to iCloud Drive, Google Drive, Dropbox or the device as plain JSON. It is readable by other software and not locked to Pryvo.
iCloud sync, no account requiredYour records sync between your own devices through iCloud. There is no Pryvo account to create and no server of ours holding your data.
